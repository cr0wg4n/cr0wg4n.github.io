summary: "Since the arrival of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Artificial Intelligence in general, the world has been taking big leaps. It is obviously not the only factor, but we are moving toward a fourth industrial revolution, and as students and professionals we had better be ready to face it. This workshop aims to explore modern tools that can automate part of our daily workflows, optimizing processes and cutting execution time. We will also cover tools that, once integrated into our everyday tasks, act as assistants or copilots and improve the quality of the final results. Throughout the workshop we will also reflect on how we use these technologies, on the role of LLMs, and on our own ability to write and express ourselves clearly."
