Paper authored by Mauricio Matias C. and Jorge Walter Orellana Araoz, published at the 2024 L Latin American Computer Conference (CLEI), Buenos Aires, Argentina.

CanSats are an excellent educational tool to introduce undergraduate students to space technologies. In regions like Bolivia, acquiring quality electronic components and high-precision PCB manufacturing can be hard and expensive. The paper presents an accessible, modular, extensible and low-cost CanSat that collects environmental data (pressure, altitude, temperature, humidity, angular velocity, acceleration and geographic position), runs pseudo-space missions and serves as educational material.
